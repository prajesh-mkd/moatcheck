# Startup Failure Reasons — Key Statistics

## Top Reasons Startups Fail (CB Insights, 2021-2025 data)

1. **No Market Need (42%)** — The #1 killer. Building something nobody wants. "If you're solving a problem that doesn't exist, no amount of engineering will save you."
2. **Ran Out of Cash (29%)** — Burned through funding before reaching profitability or next raise. Average runway at failure: 20 months.
3. **Wrong Team (23%)** — Cofounders with misaligned skills, poor hiring decisions, or team conflicts. 65% of startups fail due to co-founder conflict.
4. **Got Outcompeted (19%)** — Entered a crowded market without differentiation. Incumbents copied features faster than startups could innovate.
5. **Pricing/Cost Issues (18%)** — Couldn't find a sustainable business model. Unit economics never worked. Customer acquisition cost exceeded lifetime value.
6. **Poor Product (17%)** — Shipped too early, too buggy, or too complex. Users churned faster than they signed up.
7. **Lack of Business Model (17%)** — Had users but couldn't monetize. "If you can't explain how you make money in one sentence, you don't have a business."
8. **Poor Marketing (14%)** — Built it but nobody came. Underestimated cost of customer acquisition.
9. **Ignored Customers (14%)** — Didn't talk to users, didn't iterate based on feedback, built in a vacuum.
10. **Mistimed the Market (13%)** — Too early (market not ready) or too late (already saturated).

## Failure Rates by Stage

- **Year 1:** 20% of startups fail
- **Year 2:** 30% have failed
- **Year 3:** 50% have failed
- **Year 5:** 65% have failed
- **Year 10:** 90% have failed

## Failure Rates by Industry (5-year failure rate)

- **Restaurants/Food:** 80%
- **Retail/E-commerce:** 75%
- **Construction:** 70%
- **Social Media/Consumer Apps:** 70%
- **Food-tech/Delivery:** 60%
- **SaaS/B2B Software:** 45%
- **Fintech:** 50%
- **Healthcare/Biotech:** 55%
- **AI/ML Companies:** 50% (rising as market saturates)

## Funding vs. Failure

- **Bootstrapped startups:** 44% survive 4+ years
- **Seed-funded startups:** 40% fail before Series A
- **Series A startups:** 35% fail before Series B
- **Average time from seed to Series A:** 22 months
- **Average seed round (2024-2025):** $3.5M
- **Median revenue at Series A:** $1.5M ARR
- **Companies that pivot 1-2 times raise 2.5x more funding** than those that pivot 0 or 3+ times

## Key Insight
The most dangerous failure mode is **premature scaling** — spending heavily on growth before achieving product-market fit. 74% of startups that fail do so because of premature scaling, not because the idea was bad.
