# Market Data by Industry — TAM, Growth Rates, Key Metrics

## Technology & Software

### SaaS / Cloud Software
- **Global TAM (2025):** $250B+ | **CAGR:** 13.7%
- **Average SaaS startup metrics:** 15% month-over-month growth in first year, $1.5M ARR at Series A
- **Key metric:** Net Revenue Retention (NRR) — best companies >130%
- **Median CAC payback:** 18 months | **Target LTV:CAC ratio:** 3:1+

### Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning
- **Global TAM (2025):** $190B | **CAGR:** 36.6%
- **Enterprise AI adoption rate:** 55% (2025) up from 20% (2020)
- **Generative AI market (2025):** $65B | **CAGR:** 42%
- **Warning:** Market is hot but crowded. Most AI startups will fail due to commoditization of base models.

### Cybersecurity
- **Global TAM (2025):** $190B | **CAGR:** 12.4%
- **Average breach cost (2024):** $4.88M
- **Key insight:** Regulatory compliance drives spending. Companies MUST buy security products.

## Fintech & Financial Services

### Digital Payments
- **Global TAM (2025):** $11.6T in transaction value | **CAGR:** 11.8%
- **Mobile payments (2025):** $4.7T
- **Key insight:** Embedded payments (Stripe model) growing faster than standalone payment apps.

### Neobanking / Digital Banking
- **Global TAM (2025):** $65B | **CAGR:** 16.2%
- **Key challenge:** Unit economics are brutal. Most neobanks are unprofitable. Interchange revenue is thin.

### Insurtech
- **Global TAM (2025):** $40B | **CAGR:** 15.7%
- **Key insight:** Embedded insurance (at point of purchase) growing 6x faster than standalone insurtech.

## Healthcare & Life Sciences

### Digital Health
- **Global TAM (2025):** $370B | **CAGR:** 27.7%
- **Telehealth market:** $90B | Remote patient monitoring: $40B
- **Key insight:** Regulatory moats are strong. FDA-approved AI diagnostics have massive protection.

### Biotech / Drug Discovery
- **Avg cost to bring a drug to market:** $2.6B | **Timeline:** 10-15 years
- **Success rate from Phase 1 to approval:** 7.9%
- **AI in drug discovery market (2025):** $3.5B | **CAGR:** 40.2%

## E-commerce & Retail

### Global E-commerce
- **Global TAM (2025):** $6.3T | **CAGR:** 9.4%
- **E-commerce as % of total retail:** 21% (2025)
- **Key metrics:** Average conversion rate 2.5-3%, cart abandonment rate 70%

### Direct-to-Consumer (DTC)
- **US DTC market (2025):** $175B
- **Key challenge:** Customer acquisition costs have tripled since 2018. Facebook/Google ad costs make DTC economics increasingly difficult.

## Food & Agriculture

### Food Delivery
- **Global TAM (2025):** $370B | **CAGR:** 10.3%
- **Profit margins:** Most platforms lose money per order. DoorDash first profitable quarter in Q4 2022.
- **Key insight:** Food delivery is a scale game. Top 2-3 players in each market take 80%+ share. New entrants face near-impossible economics.

### AgriTech
- **Global TAM (2025):** $26B | **CAGR:** 12.1%
- **Key insight:** Climate change is driving massive investment. Precision agriculture, vertical farming, alternative proteins.

## Climate & Energy

### Clean Energy / Climate Tech
- **Global TAM (2025):** $1.5T | **CAGR:** 16.5%
- **EV market (2025):** $500B | Solar: $300B
- **Key insight:** Government subsidies (IRA in US) creating massive tailwinds. Hardware + regulatory moats are strong.

### Carbon Markets
- **Global TAM (2025):** $2B voluntary + $900B compliance
- **CAGR:** 30%+ for voluntary markets
- **Key insight:** Early stage, massive potential. Measurement, reporting, and verification (MRV) is the bottleneck.

## Education

### EdTech
- **Global TAM (2025):** $400B | **CAGR:** 16.3%
- **Online learning market:** $185B
- **Key challenge:** Completion rates for online courses are 3-15%. Engagement is the #1 problem.
- **AI impact:** ChatGPT is disrupting tutoring, homework help, and basic content creation.
